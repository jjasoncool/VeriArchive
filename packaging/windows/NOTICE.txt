Third-party components
----------------------
VeriArchive is dynamically linked against third-party libraries, among them GTK, GLib, Pango,
Cairo, HarfBuzz and gdk-pixbuf, which are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License
(LGPL) version 2.1 or later, and against SQLite (public domain), OpenSSL (Apache-2.0), the GCC
runtime (GPL with the GCC Runtime Library Exception) and others.

share\licenses\THIRD-PARTY.txt lists every bundled DLL and the MSYS2 package it comes from;
share\licenses\<package>\ holds that package's license. The libraries are unmodified builds from
the MSYS2 project (https://packages.msys2.org), where their source code is available. Under the
LGPL you may replace any of these DLLs with a compatible build of your own.
