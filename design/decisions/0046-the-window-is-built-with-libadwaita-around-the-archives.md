# The window is built with libadwaita around the archives

The window had seven views side by side in a GTK stack with its sidebar: Archive List, Jobs, Register Archive, Verify Archive, Compare Archives, Verification History and Settings. Each was a form of entries, check boxes and drop-downs above a report in plain text. To verify an archive, the operator went to another view and chose it in a drop-down whose first item was a folder typed above; a result was the report's text, gone once another operation ran in that view; the history showed one archive at a time; nothing showed which devices were attached, nor whether an archive was; and relocating, exporting and retiring an archive, and recording which archive another is a copy of, were the command line's alone. The owner found the window ugly and its flow poor, and asked for a modern design and its flows before any GTK work. On 2026-11-04 the owner approved an interactive prototype of it (sample data only: "原型可行 請實作") and decided five points: (1) a page Devices, and each archive's attached state, live; (2) the archive a registration is a copy of, chosen in Register Archive; (3) the history of every archive in one page, with a filter; (4) relocation, export and retirement in the window; (5) a carrier confirmed by the operator without its device's serial, recorded in the hash-chained history. Point 5 belongs to decision 0047, with the enclosure flow it needs; this record states the rest.

## Decision (operator, 2026-11-04)

1. **libadwaita ≥ 1.5 on GTK ≥ 4.14, its API pinned, and bundled with the program.** The GUI is an `AdwApplication` built on libadwaita, still through the C API from C++ (decision 0016). Its build needs `gtk4 >= 4.14` and `libadwaita-1 >= 1.5`, the versions Ubuntu 24.04 ships, the oldest of the CI's systems; where GTK4 is found without libadwaita, configuring fails rather than building no GUI. The build defines `GDK_VERSION_MIN_REQUIRED` and `GDK_VERSION_MAX_ALLOWED` as `GDK_VERSION_4_14`, and `ADW_VERSION_MIN_REQUIRED` and `ADW_VERSION_MAX_ALLOWED` as `ADW_VERSION_1_5`, so that a call of a later version warns, and a warning fails the build: the window uses no widget of a later libadwaita (AdwSpinner, AdwButtonRow, AdwBottomSheet, AdwToggleGroup, AdwWrapBox; a `GtkSpinner` turns where one is needed). The owner's condition: the program must still run when its folder is moved to another computer. The Windows package bundles libadwaita's DLL beside GTK's, its Traditional Chinese texts (`share/locale/zh_TW/LC_MESSAGES/libadwaita.mo`, so that its own words, as a dialog's, follow the interface language) and its license (`share/licenses/libadwaita`), which the package test requires; the notice names it among the LGPL libraries. CI installs it on Ubuntu (`libadwaita-1-dev`), Fedora (`libadwaita-devel`) and MSYS2 (`mingw-w64-ucrt-x86_64-libadwaita`).

2. **The window's layout.** An `AdwApplicationWindow` (1100 × 720 at first) holds a sidebar beside the page shown (`AdwNavigationSplitView`), each under a header bar of its own; a window narrower than 860sp shows one of the two at a time. Above the sidebar: VeriArchive, the name of the open repository's folder, and Register Archive (+), which opens its dialog. The sidebar's pages, in order: **Archives** (封存), **Devices** (裝置), **Jobs** (工作), **History** (歷程), **Settings** (設定), each with an icon of the theme; Jobs has the gears (`system-run-symbolic`), where the prototype drew circling arrows, which are Refresh in the head of Archives, beside it on one screen. Above each page, beside its title, the **jobs button**: a ring of how far the running jobs are, the word Jobs, the number of jobs that run or wait, and the tooltip and accessible name "Jobs: N running, M waiting"; it opens a popover of the jobs that run and wait and the last three that ended, with "See all jobs". Register Archive, Compare Archives, the relocation and a job's details are dialogs (`AdwDialog`) over the window; every question, and the folder or volume to verify, is an `AdwAlertDialog`, Cancel its default, with Abandon, Retire and Stop them and close in the colour of what cannot be undone. A short word that something started or ended, such as "ARCH-0001: FULL VERIFY PASSED" with View result, is a toast at the foot of the window. The result of a recorded run is a page of its own, over Archives or History, whose back button names the page it came from. Results, marks and alerts take their colour from the theme's named colours, so they read in the light and the dark style alike. The question on closing while jobs run (decision 0045), the notice while the repository's database is upgraded (decision 0032), the language change, which rebuilds the window and keeps what was typed and chosen (decision 0017), and the smoke test, which builds every page in either language and rebuilds the window in the other, stay.

3. **Archives** is where the operator works with archives.
   - **Unfinished operations** above the list (decision 0022): each registration, verification and relocation that has not finished, wherever it was started, with its label, root, operation, progress and state, and Resume and Abandon... as decisions 0022, 0025, 0026 and 0033 give them, every rule, refusal and question kept. A stopped relocation has Resume too (point 7).
   - **The list**: a row per archive with a dot in the colour of its state; its label and volume label; where it is attached now (its root on an attached volume, Not attached, The device differs from the one recorded, Several volumes look like this archive; point 6); its device model, capacity and "Last FULL verify 3 days ago"; the result of its last FULL verification as a pill (PASSED, PASSED_WITH_WARNINGS, FAILED; the last verification's result with its mode where it has no FULL one; Never verified), and beside it its marks: OVERDUE, its device alerts (⚠ device reports a problem, ⚠ reading slower), RETIRED, REGISTRATION_INCOMPLETE (its first registration has not finished). A manifest that cannot be used shows its problem under the row, in red; a retired archive's lost manifest, "Manifest lost: ...", not in red (decision 0028).
   - **The details** of the archive chosen, beside the list: its label, volume and root; a menu (⋯) with Compare with another archive..., Create a new revision..., Copy archive UUID, Export manifest..., Relocate to a new carrier... and Retire archive..., each greyed with the reason where it does not apply; a banner of the result of its last FULL verification with its mode ("FULL VERIFY PASSED"), when it ended in local time with its offset and how long ago, the last verification of another mode where that is later, each overdue limit that fired, and that it is retired (R-21, R-105); **FULL verify**, a split button (`AdwSplitButton`) whose menu offers "FULL verify, and add chunk hashes" only where chunk hashes can be added (decision 0033's rule), "FULL verify with strict metadata", and "Verify a folder or volume...", which verifies the archive at the root chosen; **View result** of its last FULL verification; its device alerts in red; the card of its verification or relocation while one waits or works (title, progress, the alert, Stop, Details), where a registration's job shows only in Jobs and the jobs button's popover; the notice of a stopped verification or relocation of it with Resume; and the groups Device (the carrier its current revision records, and where it is attached now), Content (root, files and size, revision and when it was recorded, chunk hashes, archive UUID), Copies (the archive it is a copy of and those that are its copies, each with Compare...), and Recent verifications, each leading to its result page, with the archive's whole history in History.

4. **The other pages.**
   - **The result page** of a run: a banner (the result with its mode, the archive, when it ended; PASSED adds "100% of the registered content was read and matched"), its device alerts, the items first where it FAILED or PASSED_WITH_WARNINGS, its figures (read and matched, entries compared, duration, average read speed), its read speed by position in the content, as a chart of each hundredth against the run it was compared with where the run timed at least 1 s of reading (point 8), its items, and the expanders Device and identity and Technical details (run, mode, revision, the run it resumed, program version, computer, R-02's sentence). An `INCOMPLETE` run says how far it came and offers Resume where it can be resumed.
   - **Devices** (owner point 1): every device attached now, with its model, bus, capacity, serial or WWN and the health it reports (decision 0044), asked once per device each time the page reads them; the volumes on each with their space; the archives whose roots lie on each volume, each leading to it in Archives; and Register this volume for a volume that holds none, or "Being registered: <root>" in its place while a registration waits or works there (a job of the window, or an unfinished registration not stopped, wherever it was started). It reads them off the window's thread while Devices or Archives shows, every 30 seconds and at Refresh, with no administrator permission, and writes nothing to them. `media list` asks each device's health through the same function.
   - **Jobs**: the jobs of the window in three groups, Running, Waiting and Ended (newest first), each with its title, progress (share, speed, time left), its alert, Stop while it waits or works, Details, and View result for an ended verification; Clear ended jobs; Stop asks nothing (decision 0045).
   - **History** (owner point 3): the recorded runs of every archive, newest first, or of the archive chosen in the filter Archive (All archives first); a row gives the result with its mode, the archive and when the run ended, what it read and matched, how long it took and how fast it read, and its notes (what else the run is, as "relocation from r1", and its device alerts) in each row; a row leads to the run's result page. Check history chain says what it found in the page, for the history of every archive, with the limits of the chain.
   - **Settings**, a preferences page: Repository (RepositoryRoot, Selected by, repository_uuid, Config file, each copy of the database kept before an upgrade, the warnings that hold, R-12's among them, and the notes of the open), then the backup reminder (R-86); Interface (the language, locked while a job waits or works, with the sentence why); Verification policy (Overdue after, Strict metadata, Trusted signing keys); About, last: the program's version and R-02's sentence (point 8). Without a repository: why none is open, R-12's warning, and Initialise repository.

5. **Register Archive and Compare Archives are dialogs.**
   - **Register Archive** has three pages. Choose the source: the attached volumes, read off the window's thread, each with its label, mount point or GUID path, device, bus, capacity and space used, chosen whole with its radio, or, where it holds archives' roots, not as a whole but with "Choose a folder in X..."; a folder chosen (`GtkFileDialog`), typed or pasted, a volume's GUID path too. Next reads it, refused as the command line refuses a root (decision 0029), and finds what lies there. Options offers only what applies there: the label (left empty, the repository's next one, which it names); Create a new revision and Register as a separate archive even though this folder or volume is registered, only where an archive is registered there; the resume of an interrupted registration of that root, the only way on while there is one; Also write a manifest copy to the media (.veriarchive), off; and **Copy of which archive (optional)** (owner point 2, decision 0005: `register --copy-of`), set where the root is a sector-by-sector clone. Confirm says what the registration will read, record and write, and Start registration runs it as a job. A registration that an older version left still asks first (decision 0022, point 7).
   - **Compare Archives**: Reference archive (A) and Copy to check (B), in which the copies of A come first and the first is chosen, A = B refused as before; Compare runs a job whose report (at most 2 000 differences listed, decision 0020) shows in its details. It opens from the details' menu, or from a copy's Compare... with both chosen.

6. **Where an archive is attached** is told from one read of the attached volumes against every archive's latest manifest, by a function of the core (`where_attached`, beside identity matching, no platform call): attached at its root where its volume matches (`MATCH`, `MATCH_WEAK`); "the device differs from the one recorded" where its filesystem is on another device (`MIGRATED`), with Relocate to this carrier... except for a retired archive; several volumes where more than one looks like it (`AMBIGUOUS`), which says to verify it by its folder; and not attached otherwise. It is runtime state, never identity (GLOSSARY: Attached), and FULL verify stays offered where the read may be old. In the screenshot mode the window shows sample devices and volumes (point 8).

7. **Relocation, export and retirement in the window** (owner point 4).
   - **Relocate to a new carrier...** opens a dialog that says first that VeriArchive copies nothing and the operator copies the archive's files with their own tools, lists the attached volumes (one that overlaps another archive's root cannot be the new root), takes the new root chosen, typed or pasted, and says what the relocation does: it reads the new carrier in full and records revision N+1 only when every entry matches revision N (decision 0004). Start relocation checks the root off the window's thread first, as the relocation will, and a refusal stays in the dialog; then the relocation runs as a job behind every job of the new carrier's devices and of the archive. Its report, with the entries that differ where the carrier does not hold the recorded content, shows in its details, and a toast says how it ended. A stopped relocation is resumed with Resume on its row under Unfinished operations, from the archive's details, or in the dialog, which Relocate to a new carrier... opens in its resume mode while one is listed, at another root where its carrier is attached at another drive letter or mount point now: exactly `archive relocate <uuid> <root> --resume` (`app::relocation_resume`). Where another operation refuses it, the window names its own ways on (`RelocateFailure::refusing`, `app::relocation_message_in_window`), as decision 0033 words the others.
   - **Export manifest...** writes the manifest of the archive's latest revision and its `identity.json` into a folder of its own, named for the archive (its label and the start of its uuid), inside the folder chosen, so that a second archive's export never replaces the first's identity file; never inside an attached archive's root, whose next FULL verification would report its files as added (refused at once from the last read of the devices, and again on the job's thread). It runs as a job with a bar of the bytes copied, which Stop cancels writing nothing (R-94; `Counting::Bytes`), and waits for no device, as a comparison. `import` stays on the command line.
   - **Retire archive...** reads first, without writing, what retiring would do (`retirement_of`), then asks, Cancel the default: what retirement means, that it cannot be undone; for an archive whose latest manifest is lost so that matching cannot use it, the command line's explanation (decision 0028, point 9) and a confirmation of its own, without which Retire is not offered. Retire then retires it with the checks again. Both run as the window's single step beside the jobs (as abandoning does), refused while a job of the window waits or works on the archive.
   - A retired archive can still be verified in the window, as by its folder and its uuid (decisions 0003, 0022 point 5).

8. **Changes in behaviour, and narrowings.**
   - **Settings shows the program's version in About, its last group** (amending decision 0037, point 4: "as its first line"): the owner approved the prototype's layout, where the version is with what the program detects.
   - **Adding chunk hashes with strict metadata** is asked for through the policy's `strict_metadata` alone: the menu of FULL verify has one item for each, where Verify Archive's two check boxes could be ticked together. Every other verification's strict metadata, the policy or the menu's item, is as before.
   - **Retiring waits for every operation that would give the archive a new revision**: an unfinished registration (its first one included), relocation or verification that adds chunk hashes refuses `archive retire` and Retire archive... alike (exit `12`), in the words in which such an operation meets a registration (decisions 0025, 0026, 0030, 0033), and in the window's words there. It waited before only for a registration, and only for an archive whose latest manifest is lost (decision 0028, point 9). A relocation and a registration that finish now find their archive still registered in the transaction that records the revision, and otherwise record nothing ("archive U was retired while this relocation ran; a retired archive takes no new revisions", and so for a registration): a relocation that ran while the archive was retired had set it back to registered.
   - **A registration or relocation of an archive retired since cannot be resumed**: its row reads "archive is retired; abandon it", with Abandon... and no Resume, where only a registration over a lost manifest read so (decision 0028); `register <root> --resume` refuses it, naming `archive abandon-registration` (exit `12`), and `archive relocate --resume` as it refuses every retired archive.
   - **A relocation and an export are jobs** (decision 0045), each keeping the computer awake while it works and told in a notification when it ends while no window of the program is active (decision 0043), as a registration, verification and comparison are.
   - **Retirement and its preview take no cancel and no progress** (an exception to CLAUDE.md's rule 7, R-94): neither is long. They write one row, and read at most the archive's latest manifest whole, the read `load_candidate_pool` makes without cancel or progress for every refresh of Archives, verification, registration and relocation; honouring a cancel there would change that read for all of them. The window shows a moving bar while they run.
   - **Long lists in steps**: a result page lists its items, and History its runs, 200 at a time, with "Show N more", so that a run that records every entry of a large archive, or years of history, never builds tens of thousands of rows at once; nothing is left out.
   - **The read speed chart** is drawn only for a run that timed at least 1 s of reading (`chart_least_milliseconds`): a small archive, read in milliseconds, gives speeds that say nothing; the page then says that the verification read too little to chart, and still gives the read speed and its comparison.
   - **The read speed's comparison names when the compared run finished**, in the window's report and page and on the command line: "Read speed: 182 MiB/s; the last FULL verification of this archive on this device, which finished 2026-11-01 14:03:00 (UTC+8), read 180 MiB/s", where it ended with the date in brackets (decision 0044).
   - **The screenshot mode shows no device or volume of this computer but through the sample identity**: Devices, Register Archive and the relocation dialog list a fixed list of sample devices and volumes (`SAMPLE-DEVICE-01`, `/media/SAMPLE-VOLUME-1`, …, with sample healths), no device is asked for its health, the volume of the sample folders is shown as the sample identity, and a result page names the computer that recorded a run `SAMPLE-HOST`.
   - **Terms renamed** (decision 0017): the pages Archives (封存, where 0017 said Archive List 封存列表) and History (歷程, where it said Verification History 驗證歷程), whose old entries left the catalog; "Archive UUID" (封存 UUID); the result page's "Read and matched" (已讀取且相符, the coverage) and "Entries compared" (已比對的項目, GLOSSARY Entry); the menu item "FULL verify, and add chunk hashes" (完整驗證，並加入區塊雜湊), where 0033 named the checkbox "Full verify, and add chunk hashes". Every text of the app that named a control of the old views is reworded for the new ones in both languages (`app::refusal_in_window`, the verify report's hint on an interrupted run and its advice on Unbaselined entries, the refusals of a verification and a registration, the MIGRATED notices): where decision 0033 names "Resume interrupted run once the archive is chosen above" and "ticking Resume an interrupted registration of this folder or volume and pressing Register", the window now says Resume in the archive's details and "choosing its root in Register Archive, whose options then resume it".
   - **Verify Archive's Resume interrupted run is gone**: the details' notice of the archive's stopped verification, one that adds chunk hashes included, and its row under Unfinished operations continue it, also after the window that ran it was closed (`app::run_to_resume` and `app::resumable_adding_verification` are no longer used and were removed).
   - **History's notes in every row**: decision 0026's column Note, shown only for an archive with a relocation's runs, is the notes of each row, with the run's device alerts (decision 0044).
9. No change to the manifests, the database schema or the golden manifests, and no new `--json` member: the state `archive_retired` that `archive list|show --json` already gave a registration over a lost manifest now also marks any registration or relocation of an archive retired since (point 8).

## Why

The owner judged the window by its look and its flow, and approved the prototype after trying it: the archives in front, each with its state and its next step beside it, the operations as jobs, a page per run. libadwaita gives that layout on GTK's C API, as the systems' own programs have it: the adaptive split view, navigation pages with their own back button, preferences pages, split buttons, toasts and the platform's dialogs, in the light and the dark style. 1.5 is what Ubuntu 24.04 ships, the oldest system CI builds on; pinning the API there keeps a newer local libadwaita (the development machine has 1.10) from slipping in calls that the CI and the package cannot run. The owner asked that the program still run on another computer once its folder is moved, so libadwaita is bundled as GTK is.

The obligations of §B17 move, none goes (the Consequences' HANDOFF §B17 item lists where each lives). Relocation, export and retirement come into the window because the owner decided so (point 4); relocation is the operator's way on after a disk is copied or an enclosure changes, and it reads the whole new carrier as a FULL verification does, so it belongs with the jobs. Retiring had to wait for every operation that would give the archive a new revision once the window can run them beside it: such an operation, finishing, recorded its revision over the retirement and set the archive back to registered.

Rejected alternatives:

- Plain GTK4 restyled, a `GtkStack` with its sidebar as before: no adaptive split view, no navigation pages with their back button, no toasts nor dialogs of the platform's look, and every widget's style written by hand.
- libadwaita 1.6 or later, for AdwSpinner, AdwButtonRow or AdwBottomSheet: Ubuntu 24.04 ships 1.5.
- A C++ binding of libadwaita or GTK: decision 0016's reasons.
- Requiring libadwaita to be installed beside the program on Windows: the package runs without MSYS2 (R-121), and the owner moves the folder between computers.
- Tables of columns for Archives and History, as before: the owner chose rows with results and marks and a details pane, as the prototype shows them, and a table for a narrow window cannot collapse.
- Keeping Verify Archive beside the details: two ways to start the same verification, one of them choosing the archive in a drop-down.
- A fourth item in the menu of FULL verify for adding chunk hashes with strict metadata: it is the menu used most, and the policy already asks for strict metadata in every verification.
- Exporting into the folder chosen, as `export --out` does: a second archive's `identity.json` would replace the first's.
- Retiring as a job: it reads one manifest and writes one row; refused while a job works on the archive, it never waits for a device.
- Asking before Stop: decision 0045 asks only when the window closes, and a stopped job is resumed.
- The operator's confirmation of a carrier without its serial (owner point 5), and the enclosure flow, here: they need a new member of the hash-chained history and the question of elevation, decision 0047's.
